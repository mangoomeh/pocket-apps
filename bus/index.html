    Where the times come from

    Arrival times are LTA's live data. By default they come through arrivelah, a free public service run by busrouter.sg; if it's down, times won't load. For dependable times, run your own free server with your own LTA key: see the steps in tools/bus-worker.js, then paste its address here.
