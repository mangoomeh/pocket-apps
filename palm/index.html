How to read your palm

  1. Use good light, with your hand relaxed and slightly cupped so the lines show.
  2. Start with your dominant hand (the one you write with); you can read the other one afterwards and compare.
  3. Look for the three big lines first: heart (top), head (middle) and life (round the thumb). Most people have all three.
  4. Lines vary a lot and many hands are in between. When unsure, pick the closer match or skip.

Palmistry is a tradition, not a science: enjoy it as a way to reflect, not to predict.
